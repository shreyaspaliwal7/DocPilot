Acme Technologies Employee Handbook and Policies

1. Remote Work Policy
Employees are eligible for hybrid remote work after completing 3 months of tenure. Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM EST, during which all team members must be reachable on chat and email.

2. Annual Leave and Sick Time
All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual vacation leave and 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A maximum of 5 unused vacation days may roll over to the next calendar year.

3. Home Office Equipment Stipend
Full-time employees receive a $500 one-time equipment stipend for home office setup upon hire. This budget is eligible for renewal every 2 years.

4. Health and Wellness Benefits
Comprehensive health coverage is provided through the Apex Health Premier plan, which includes medical, dental, and vision care. Acme Technologies covers 80% of the employee premium costs.

5. Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement
Business travel requires pre-approval. Meals during business travel are capped at $75 per day. Standard hotel accommodation is capped at $200 per night. Itemized receipts are strictly required for any individual expense above $25.

6. Probationary Period
All new full-time hires undergo a standard probationary period of 90 days. A formal check-in is conducted at 30 days, followed by a final evaluation at 90 days.

7. Performance Reviews and Merit Increases
Performance evaluations are held biannually in June and December. Any approved merit salary adjustments take effect at the start of the following quarter.

8. Employee Referral Bonus Program
Acme offers an employee referral bonus of $2,500 for successfully hiring a senior engineering role, and $1,500 for all other full-time roles. The bonus is disbursed after the referred hire completes 90 days of employment.

9. Resignation and Notice Period
The standard notice period for voluntary resignation is 2 weeks. Director and executive level positions require a minimum notice period of 4 weeks.

10. Learning and Professional Development
Each full-time team member is allocated an annual learning and development stipend of $1,000 to cover professional conferences, books, and technical certifications.
